## 📊 ALGORITHM MASTERY 2026

### TikTok Algorithm (FYP Focus)

**How it works**:
- Tests videos on small audiences first → strong early performance = broader FYP push
- Processes thousands of signals via ML to predict engagement
- Updates recommendations continuously based on user behavior

**Ranking Factor Hierarchy (2026)**:

1. **SAVES/SHARES** (Highest priority) → Intent to return/distribute
2. **Video completion** (especially 5+ second "Qualified Views" for quality signal)
3. **Watch time** (finishing long videos = strong resonance)
4. **Likes** (lower weight than saves/shares now)
5. **Comments** (engagement signal)
6. **Follows from video** (strong interest indicator)
7. **Rewatches** (indicates compelling content)
8. **Search value** (matching spoken/text content to queries)
9. **Topic authority** (consistent engagement with creator/topic)
10. **Hashtags/sounds** (discovery signals)
11. **Captions/text overlays** (TikTok SEO optimization)
12. **Device/location** (lowest weight, just for cold start)

**What kills reach**:
- ❌ Weak 3-second hook (viewers scroll immediately)
- ❌ Drop-offs before 5 seconds (no "Qualified View")
- ❌ Duplicate content or spam
- ❌ Watermarks from other platforms
- ❌ Under-review flagged content
- ❌ Low shareability (not worth sending to friends)

**2026 Updates**:
- **5-second "Qualified Views"** now critical (extended from 3s)
- **TikTok SEO** emphasized (optimize captions/overlays for search)
- **Photo carousels** use "Swipe-Through Rate" (STR) like video completion
- **Post timing** matters for initial engagement bursts

---

### Instagram Reels Algorithm

**How it works**:
- "Entertainment-first" approach prioritizing non-follower content via Explore/Reels
- Gathers pool of potential videos → scores based on user activity → orders for max engagement
- Predicts: will user spend 10+ seconds viewing, comment, reshare, visit profile, follow?

**Ranking Signal Hierarchy (2026)**:

**For FOLLOWERS (Connected Reach)**:
1. **Likes per reach** (primary signal)
2. **Watch time** (full watches + rewatches)
3. **Comments** (engagement depth)
4. **Saves** (intent to return)
5. **Profile visits** (interest in creator)

**For NON-FOLLOWERS (Unconnected Reach - KEY for growth)**:
1. **Shares per reach** (critical — shareability = virality)
2. **3-second hook strength** (prevents scroll)
3. **Watch time** (completion rate)
4. **Trending audio** (discovery boost)
5. **Pattern interrupts** (cuts, movement, face in frame)

**Other factors**:
- User's interaction history with similar content
- Recency (fresh content prioritized)
- Topic alignment (via "Your Algorithm" tool - users can add/remove topics)
- Engagement speed (quick likes/comments = hot content)

**What kills reach**:
- ❌ Early drop-offs in first 3 seconds
- ❌ Low completion rate
- ❌ Watermarks (Instagram penalizes TikTok watermarks)
- ❌ Low-quality resolution
- ❌ Topic mismatch (users filter you out via "Your Algorithm")
- ❌ >90 second videos (completion suffers)

**2026 Updates**:
- **"Your Algorithm" control** (Dec 2025 launch) - users curate topics, reducing mismatch penalties
- **All video posts auto-upload as Reels** for priority
- **Captions boost retention +38%** (accessibility + engagement)
- **Shares/DMs** now weighted heavier for non-follower reach

### Retention Techniques

**First 3 seconds** (71% of retention decided here):
- ❌ "Hey guys, welcome back..." (INSTANT SCROLL)
- ✅ Jump straight into value/shock/question
- ✅ Face in frame + movement + text
- ✅ Pattern interrupt (audio cut, visual glitch, sudden zoom)

**Throughout video**:
- **Cut every 1.5-3 seconds** (brain can't predict next frame = keeps watching)
- **Text overlays** (dual-channel processing - eyes + ears engaged)
- **Progress indication** ("1/3 secrets revealed...")
- **Cliffhangers** ("But wait, watch what happens next...")
- **Visual variety** (close-ups, wide shots, POV changes, before/afters)

**Looping for rewatches**:
- End with "Did you catch [detail]?" (forces rewatch)
- Seamless loop (last frame → first frame transition)
- "Watch again for the hidden prompt" (scavenger hunt)

## 🔥 EMERGENCY PLAYBOOK

### If stuck at <200 views ("View Jail")

**Diagnosis**: Algorithm doesn't understand your niche yet

**Fix**:
1. Niche down HARD (only AI video content, no random posts)
2. Post 5 videos/day for 7 days (flood the algorithm with data)
3. Use hyper-targeted hashtags: #AIvideo #AIcinematic #KlingAI (not #fyp #viral)
4. Engage 30 min/day in your niche (like, comment, duet)
5. Delete lowest-performing 20% of content (confuses algorithm)

---

### If growth stalls (plateau after initial spike)

**Diagnosis**: Format fatigue or algorithm shift

**Fix**:
1. Change hook style completely (try 3 new templates)
2. Test new content pillar (if doing tutorials, switch to entertainment)
3. Ride trending sounds within 24 hours (TikTok Creative Center)
4. Collaborate with creators 1-2 tiers above you
5. Analyze competitors who ARE growing (steal their format)
6. Post at different times (test new windows)

---

### If losing followers (bad content/wrong audience)

**Diagnosis**: Content doesn't deliver on profile promise

**Fix**:
1. Check which videos caused unfollows (TikTok analytics shows this)
2. Return to core niche (AI cinematic content only)
3. Audit bio/profile - does it match content?
4. Delete off-brand content
5. Post 3 "greatest hits" variations in a row (remind followers why they followed)
